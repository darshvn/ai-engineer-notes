# Next-Token Field Notes

Study notes on the six conceptual foundations of large language models — language modeling, next-token prediction, why prediction yields reasoning, scaling laws and emergence, foundation vs task-specific models, and the generative/representation split.

Each idea appears twice: once in prose, once as a diagram of the mechanism. Ends with a retrieval-practice bank and a spacing schedule.
